*This is a reader-submitted account, originally shared anonymously on Reddit. We have not verified it. It refers to a possible sexual assault and may be distressing.*

I was 16 at the time and had just barely gotten my license. I had a friend at school, a girl most people considered "boy crazy", but to my disappointment she wasn't interested in me. She was the typical popular, good-looking girl who was into jocks, and I was the nerdiest kid in the class. But we were very close, and she would tell me all her struggles and then delete the messages, mostly because the guys she liked were very possessive.

In the dead of night in the winter, I got a call from her. This was very unusual, mostly because she never called, but also because it was a school night and very late. She was speaking in a hushed, shaking voice and asked me to come pick her up. I knew something was wrong, but she had to hang up after giving me the address.

This was before GPS, and I barely knew how to get to school. I printed out a MapQuest route to her boyfriend's house in a different city. She had asked me to bring her clothes, specifically a shirt, underwear and some sweats. I pushed my car out of the driveway and coasted until I was sure my parents couldn't hear it.

I got to the house and she came out, obviously trying to make as little noise as possible.

She was naked. Her makeup was smeared from crying. Her hair was matted.

She got in the car, told me to take off, and put the clothes on. I asked her what happened, and all she could say without breaking down was that she had gone to her boyfriend's birthday party and woken up like this, with no memory. She woke up in the middle of the room with all his friends there, and him also naked.

I took her home and snuck back into my house two hours before I had to get up for school. We never spoke of it again. I didn't think much of it then, but now, 17 years later, I have my own kids, including a daughter who is the light of my world, and I can't tell this story without crying.

She has been married with kids to a seemingly good guy for a decade. We don't talk anymore, but I still check her Facebook from time to time, and I hope she's okay now.
