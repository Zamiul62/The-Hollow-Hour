## Confirmed

In early 1959 a group of experienced hikers died in the northern Urals. Their tent was found cut open from the inside, and several were found without proper clothing.

## Debated

Avalanche, slab-wind and other natural explanations have been proposed, and investigators reopened the case in the 2010s. Treat any single-cause claim with caution.

## Speculation

Many popular theories lack evidence. We label them as speculation and do not present them as findings.
