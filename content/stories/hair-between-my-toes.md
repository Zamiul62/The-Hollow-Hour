*This is a reader-submitted account, originally shared anonymously on Reddit. We have not verified it. It describes the death of a child.*

I was about 7 or 8 years old when I went with my family to a man-made lake family park in South Georgia. It was basically a big, dug-out pond that the owner had surrounded with beach sand and palm trees. He charged admission for people to come, park their cars, grill and swim for the day. It also had a big, unsafe-as-hell waterslide that kids got hurt on.

Where my family would park was in front of a little island, a clump of sand about 30 yards off the shoreline. I was not the best swimmer, but the water was just above my head, so I could bob up and down and bounce out to the island.

One day, while bouncing out to that little island, my foot came down onto what felt like a face. I felt what seemed to be a nose against my toe. The thing I remember most is the feeling of hair flowing between my toes, that feeling of fine hair waving in the water.

I freaked out and sat on the shore for the longest time. I was a little kid and didn't think anything more of it.

I can still remember the scared parents going around yelling a little boy's name, asking if anyone had seen him. They pulled his body from under the water right in front of that island.

I'm 47 now, and I can still remember the feeling of that hair between my toes.
