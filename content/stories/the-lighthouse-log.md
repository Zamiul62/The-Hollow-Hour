*This is a work of fiction written for The Hollow Hour.*

The relief crew found the logbook open on the desk, the ink dry. Every entry was neat until the last page.

## Day 41

"The light turns on its own now. I stopped winding it on Tuesday. The lamp does not care."

## Day 44

"There are more steps than there were. I counted 112 going up and 131 coming down."

## The last line

"Someone is climbing, and they are counting along with"

The entry stops there. The crew never agreed on how many steps they climbed to reach the lamp room.
