*This is a reader-submitted account, originally shared anonymously on Reddit. We have not verified it.*

My grandparents are Dutch immigrants in New Zealand, and in the 1970s they had a caravan that they took camping to a rural part of the North Island for a little family fun. My mum and her siblings would have been 10 or younger at the time.

They were way out in the middle of nowhere, near a lake in the rainforest. After the kids had gone to sleep, my grandparents stayed awake a little while, reading and having a cup of tea.

They started hearing strange noises, like footsteps outside and twigs breaking, maybe a hushed voice, but they thought they were just being paranoid. They were getting a bit freaked out at this point, but assumed it was nothing and that they'd be safe.

Grandpa told me he couldn't go to sleep, and then he heard a definite footstep right outside the caravan door. He peeked out the window of the caravan. It was completely pitch black, with no moonlight whatsoever, but he could see the glowing cigarette of what must have been a pretty tall man standing right at the door. He could still hear other footsteps too, even though whoever was smoking this cigarette outside the door was standing dead still.

He told me he'd never been so scared in his life, but he had to do something because he had his wife and four little kids sleeping inside the caravan. So he grabbed his butcher's knife (he was a butcher by profession, lord knows why he had it on a family camping trip) and opened the door to confront or yell or do whatever he could to scare the people away. When he opened the door he saw no one and heard no noise.

He thought he was going crazy, but he saw the cigarette butt on the ground outside the door, freshly finished.

He got into the car straight away (he had everything ready to go, luckily, because he had already planned on driving off early the next morning anyway) and drove everyone away. He found somewhere else near a town to stay instead.

This story freaks me out every time I think about it.
