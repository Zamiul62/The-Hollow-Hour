The key card opened a door between 412 and 414. There was no room number, only a faint rectangle on the wallpaper where a plate had been.

Inside, the bed was made for a guest who had already left. The TV hummed a station that did not exist, and my name was written on the notepad in my own handwriting.

I checked out early. The clerk said my stay had not been booked, and my room had been cancelled a week ago.
