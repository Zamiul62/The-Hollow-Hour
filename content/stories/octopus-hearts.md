*Demonstration article.*

Octopuses have three hearts: two serve the gills and one serves the body. Their blood uses copper-based hemocyanin instead of iron-based hemoglobin, which makes it look blue.

## Why it matters

Hemocyanin works well in cold, low-oxygen water.
